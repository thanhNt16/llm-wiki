# Meeting notes — Platform sync, 2026-09-10

Attendees: analytics lead, orders team lead, staff eng

- Recapped the attribution decision: production uses a **30-day** click
  lookback window per ADR-019. (Note: these notes were drafted before the
  September change circulated; nobody corrected them in the meeting.)
- Orders: we will use a **transactional outbox** for order event delivery —
  the durability problems with Redis pub/sub (lost messages on subscriber
  disconnect) were the deciding factor.
- Payment gateway ops reports the effective retry count in production config
  is **5**; someone should reconcile that with ADR-007's 3.
